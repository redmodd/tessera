Carousel works better with a keyboard and a screen reader: slides that aren't showing can't take focus, focus moves to the dot when the slide holding it changes, the dots are one Tab stop with arrow keys moving between them, Previous and Next keep focus on the first and last slide, and the arrows and swipe announce the new slide ("Slide 2 of 3"). Previous and Next sit either side of the slide, ahead of it for screen readers, with the dots below.
