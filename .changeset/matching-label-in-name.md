---
'tessera-learn': patch
---

Matching and Sorting buttons take their accessible name from their visible text, so screen reader and voice control users hear and say what they see. Both announce the pending selection and, once feedback shows, whether each match or placement is correct. Locked Matching items no longer offer to unmatch, and locked Sorting targets leave the tab order. Sorting targets no longer nest the "return to deck" buttons inside another button, and returning a card to the deck clears the selection and moves keyboard focus to it.
