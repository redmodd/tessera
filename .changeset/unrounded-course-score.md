Quiz, page and course scores are now kept to 2 decimal places, and pass/fail is judged on the same score the LMS is sent. This can change a result: a course average of 69.67 against a pass mark of 70 now reports 69.67 with `failed`, instead of 70 with `failed`, and 2 of 3 correct now scores 66.67, which no longer meets a pass mark of 67.
