A fill-in or long-fill-in answer or response the LMS would misread as a delimiter or reject, such as one starting with `{lang=`, `{case_matters=` or `{order_matters=` or with a `[,]` part that does, is left out of the SCORM 2004, cmi5 and xAPI record with a warning. Grading still uses it.

cmi5 and xAPI leave out a reversed correct range, with a warning, as SCORM 2004 does.
